Privacy Policy – Safe to Sign

Effective Date: April 25, 2026

Safe to Sign respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information Collection

Safe to Sign does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name or email address.

Any text or content you input into the app (such as contracts or agreements) is used solely to provide analysis results.

Before any analysis occurs, users are shown a consent prompt explaining that their contract text will be sent to a secure third-party AI service. Users must explicitly agree before any data is transmitted.

The data is processed in real-time and is not stored by Safe to Sign after analysis is complete.

2. Data Usage

Safe to Sign sends user-provided contract text to a secure third-party AI service for analysis. This data is used only to generate results and is not stored or retained by Safe to Sign after processing.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party AI services (such as OpenAI or similar providers) to analyze contract content. These services process the data solely for generating results and do not use it for unrelated purposes.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Safe to Sign may display advertisements through third-party services such as Google AdMob.

These services may collect certain information automatically, including:
- Device information (device type, operating system)
- IP address
- App usage data (such as time spent in the app or interactions with ads)

This data may be used to:
- Deliver personalized or non-personalized ads
- Measure ad performance
- Improve ad relevance

You can control ad personalization through your device settings.

For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information.

Information may only be shared when required to:
- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect users and the integrity of the service

5. User Rights

You have control over the information you provide. Since Safe to Sign does not store personal data, there is no stored account data to modify or delete.

6. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected with a revised effective date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact:
